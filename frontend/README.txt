MAA AARANYA TENT & DECORATIONS WEBSITE

HOW TO OPEN:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the extracted folder: maa_aaranya_website
3. Double-click index.html to open the website in Chrome.

FILES:
- index.html  = website content
- style.css   = design and colours
- script.js   = menu and WhatsApp booking form
- images/     = your uploaded decoration photos

IMPORTANT:
Keep the images folder beside index.html, style.css and script.js.
Do not rename the image files unless you also update their paths in index.html / style.css.

To edit business phone/address/Instagram, search those details in index.html.
